What Sunfolio does

Sunfolio finds the documents on your phone and opens them for reading: PDF files, Word files (.docx), text files and images. Its PDF tools turn pictures or scanned pages into PDFs, merge, split and compress PDFs, rotate, reorder or delete pages, and add or remove a PDF password. It works on your phone, without an account and without uploading your documents. Word files are shown in a reading view, so their layout can differ from Microsoft Word. Some file types (such as Excel, PowerPoint and older .doc files) can be listed but are opened with another app.

Your documents

Your documents stay yours and stay on your phone. When you rename, delete or share a document in Sunfolio, the app does it on your phone at your request. A deleted file cannot be recovered by Sunfolio, so check before you confirm. Use Sunfolio only with documents you have the right to open.

What it can and can't do

Sunfolio tries to open damaged, unusual or password-protected files, but it cannot promise that every file opens, that every file looks exactly as in the app that created it, or that search finds every word (for example in scanned pages, which contain pictures rather than text). Don't rely on it where a wrong or missing page could cause harm.

The PDF tools save their result as a new file and leave your original as it was, so check the result before you delete the original. Compressing a PDF can lower the quality of its pictures. If you forget a password you set on a PDF, Sunfolio cannot recover it.
